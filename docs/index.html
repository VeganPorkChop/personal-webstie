About

Building toward aerospace, one system at a time

I'm a second-year mechanical engineering student at Northwestern University, pursuing the aerospace concentration and a minor in artificial intelligence. I'm drawn to flight systems, where structures, propulsion, aerodynamics and controls all have to work together, and that is where I want to build my career. The AI minor builds on the reinforcement-learning and signal-processing work in my code projects.

I'm most at home on projects that cross disciplines: I model parts in CAD, check them with simulation, fabricate them with 3D printing and laser cutting, wire the electronics, and write the control code that makes them move.

My project write-ups document what went wrong as well as what worked, from undersized stepper motors to burned-out motor drivers, because that is where most of the engineering happened.
